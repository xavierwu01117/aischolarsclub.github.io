About

Use AI to understand, not to shortcut

We teach BHS students how to use AI tools to study smarter, focusing on using AI to actually understand material, responsibly and within school rules.

Study guides

Turn a unit's notes into a clear guide, then check it for mistakes.

Quizzes and flashcards

Make practice tests before exams and learn to spot bad questions.

Organized classes

Keep each class's syllabus and notes in one place you can ask questions about.

Build things

Learn vibe coding and Claude Code, then build a learning website with your group.

Main project

Build a learning website with your group

Groups of 2–3 design and build a website that helps students learn a topic from a BHS class, like a biology unit, a history period, or Spanish vocabulary. You'll use vibe coding and Claude Code and learn how websites work along the way. At the end, every group presents at a showcase.

Every site includes

  • A clear explanation of the topic, written and fact-checked by the group
  • At least one interactive feature: a quiz, flashcards, or a review game
  • A clean design that works on a phone
  • A "How we built it" page about what AI did and didn't do

Project rules

  • Every member can explain how their site works
  • Content is accurate and original
  • Builds use the advisor's account or school-approved tools

For members

Ground rules

Every member agrees to these. They keep the club useful and keep everyone out of trouble.

Follow each teacher's AI rules

When you're not sure what's allowed, ask the teacher.

Learn, don't outsource

Use AI to explain, quiz, and review. Never turn in AI-written work as your own.

Always check

Compare AI answers to your notes or textbook. AI can be confidently wrong.

Protect privacy

No other students' names, grades, or personal info in AI tools. Only upload class materials you're allowed to share.

Accounts

Claude accounts are 18+. Club demos run from the advisor's account, and members practice with tools the school approves.
